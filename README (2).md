# Student Budget Literacy & Fiscal Comprehension Audit (N = 40)

An empirical field study and quantitative data audit evaluating awareness versus actual conceptual understanding of Union Budget and macroeconomic terminology among undergraduate students at **Bharat College of Arts & Commerce (University of Mumbai)**.

* **Project Lead:** Dhruv Chhaganlal Shah (4-Member Research Team: Dhruv C. Shah, Rani Singh, Sneha Verma, Raj Zanje)
* **Academic Context:** Economics Field Project (Academic Year 2025-2026)
* **Sample Size:** 40 Undergraduate Students (20 Commerce vs. 20 Computer Science)
* **Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, Data Cleaning), SQL, Python (`pandas`, `matplotlib`)

## Key Quantitative Findings

| Academic Stream | Sample Size (`n`) | Term Awareness (%) | Conceptual Understanding (%) | Avg. Quiz Score (out of 10) | Primary Information Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commerce (`B.Com`)** | 20 | **90.0%** (18/20) | **45.0%** (9/20) | **6.45 / 10** | Curriculum Textbooks & Financial News |
| **Computer Science (`B.Sc CS`)** | 20 | **45.0%** (9/20) | **5.0%** (1/20) | **3.15 / 10** | Social Media Reels & Family Discussions |
| **Total / Overall** | **40** | **67.5%** (27/40) | **25.0%** (10/40) | **4.80 / 10** | Mixed Formal & Informal Channels |

* **Overall Breakdown (`N = 40`):** 25.0% Correct (10), 42.5% Partial/Confused (17), 32.5% No Knowledge (13)
